# Optional editorial pattern lens

A word or punctuation choice alone is not a defect. Do not add missing evidence to make an edit work; flag unsupported claims instead of inventing sources or silently deleting factual content.

## Content

3. **Superficial participial phrases.** Check whether “highlighting,” “ensuring,” or “reflecting” adds an unsupported conclusion. Remove only redundant framing without losing a claim; retain real causal or temporal meaning.
5. **Vague attribution.** Flag “experts believe” when the source is missing. Name a source only if supplied and authorized; preserve attribution and uncertainty rather than substituting invented authority.

## Language

7. **Inflated vocabulary.** Prefer a plainer equivalent when words such as “pivotal” or “tapestry” obscure the point. Technical terms and the author's register remain valid.
8. **Indirect copulas.** “Is” or “has” may clarify “serves as” or “boasts”; keep distinctions in function or possession.
9. **Formulaic contrasts.** Repeated “not just X, but Y” may be shortened without erasing an actual contrast.
10. **Forced triads.** Use the natural number of ideas; operational lists and genuine groups of three are fine.
11. **Synonym cycling.** Repeat a stable term when synonyms imply different entities. Preserve genuine distinctions.
12. **False ranges.** Use a direct list if “from X to Y” implies a scale that does not exist.

## Style

13. **Dash overuse.** Reduce monotonous interruption only when it impairs reading. Em dashes, en dashes, parentheses, and hyphens are legitimate; no blanket ban or mechanical substitution.
14. **Colon overuse.** Revise repetitive or unclear connectors, not valid introductions, explanations, ratios, or syntax.
15. **Boldface overuse.** Reduce decorative emphasis; preserve semantic emphasis and required format.
16. **Inline-header lists.** Remove redundant label-restatement pairs; useful lead-ins and scannable operational lists stay.
17. **Heading case.** Follow the document's house style; sentence case is optional, not universal.
18. **Decorative emoji.** Remove only unwanted decoration; preserve meaningful symbols and authorial voice.
19. **Quotation typography.** Follow explicit house style only outside protected spans. Curly quotes are not a defect and must not be mechanically straightened.

## Communication and filler

20. **Chatbot wrappers.** Remove irrelevant assistance boilerplate, not quoted dialogue or meaningful correspondence.
22. **Sycophancy.** Remove empty praise without changing a genuine stance or response.
23. **Filler.** Shorten phrases such as “in order to” where meaning and emphasis survive.
24. **Stacked hedges.** Simplify redundant hedges, never the uncertainty or risk level they express.
25. **Generic conclusions.** Trim empty outlooks; use concrete plans only when already supported. Preserve substantive conclusions.

## Plain speech

26. **Abstract metaphor nouns.** Prefer a concrete equivalent for gratuitous “nexus,” “flywheel,” or similar jargon. Keep established terms such as “API surface” when they are precise.
27. **Mechanism over promotion.** Replace vague benefit language with an observable behavior only when that behavior is supplied. Otherwise flag the evidence gap; do not fabricate metrics or cut a real claim.
28. **Dense sentences.** Split when comprehension improves, retaining logical relationships and qualifications.
29. **Active voice.** Name the actor only when known and relevant. Passive voice can be the clearest, safest choice.
30. **Weak modifiers.** Remove redundant adverbs or use supplied measurements; do not invent a stronger claim or measured delta.
31. **Plain words.** Prefer “use” to “utilize” when register and domain meaning permit.
32. **Mannered rhetoric.** Reduce theatrical openers, manufactured drama, aphorisms, and gratuitous personification when they obscure the point; keep intentional authorial expression.
33. **Over-compression.** Restore readable articles and verbs where fragments or symbol-speak impede prose. Keep code, notation, tables, and deliberate stylistic fragments intact.
